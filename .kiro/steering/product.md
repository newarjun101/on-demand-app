# Product Overview

Local Services Marketplace (`on-demand-app`) is an on-demand local services marketplace connecting Customers, Workers, and Platform Administrators.

- **Customers** (`customer-web`): Discover services/workers, create service requests, compare/accept quotes, track bookings, pay, chat, review, and open disputes.
- **Workers** (`worker-web`): Complete KYC onboarding, configure offered services/availability/radius, submit quotes, execute jobs, track earnings, and purchase visibility boosts.
- **Admins** (`admin-web`): Manage users, workers, KYC approvals, service catalog, RBAC roles, disputes, reviews, and platform configuration.
